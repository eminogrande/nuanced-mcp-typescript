## Conclusion

The lexical graph adds access to transcripts but weakens first-result ranking. The local `qwen3-embedding:0.6b` hybrid exceeds the 90% recall@5 release gate, improves MRR by 0.138, and keeps forbidden hits at zero. Its roughly 202 ms mean latency is acceptable for agent recall and remains completely outside DICTATOR's recording and paste path. The remaining top-five misses are response-style preference and project-delivery evidence.
