## Conclusion

The graph adds access to transcripts and improves recall@5, German retrieval, and current-state retrieval. It does not improve first-result ranking, remains weak on preferences and cross-language questions, and spends about 25 times more search time than the memory-only baseline. This supports replacing generic token-overlap relationships with semantic extraction, multilingual retrieval, constrained graph expansion, and reranking.
